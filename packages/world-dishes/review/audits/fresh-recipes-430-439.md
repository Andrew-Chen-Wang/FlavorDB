No dishes removed or recipes dropped. Sauces are prepared foods. Many extracted ingredient lists belonged to the first structured variant, while steps came from a later historical variant or consisted only of serving notes. Fixed recipes restore the method that matches the imported ingredient list. Salsa brava instead imported the second variant's ingredients, so its correction retains that variant's mixing instruction. Tomato sauce also restores the salt, sugar and pepper amounts omitted from the ingredient extraction.

Country choices follow the dish, including French classical sauces whose names translate as Italian, Hungarian, Roman or Portuguese. Generic sauces remain without countries where a cookbook citation alone would not establish an association. Shared associations are inclusive, including Cumberland, mint, marinière, Polish, rémoulade, Reform, Russian, soy, Thousand Island and Zingara sauces. All country overrides require the separate second-opinion pass requested for the full review.

JSON parsing and positional dish count/order were checked mechanically for all ten verdict files after writing.
