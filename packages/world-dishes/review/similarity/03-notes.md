## Proposed vocabulary (literal strings used in output)

Forms need their parent relationships added before application; these are proposals, not assumed existing vocabulary:

- `baked-cake`: [`baked`, `sweets`]. Cakes are not pastry pies or quick breads; used for carrot cake, Christmas cake and coffee cake.
- `salad`: [`cold-dish`]. Dressed shredded raw carrot is neither raw meat cubes nor sliced meat.
- `savory-custard`: [`steamed`], with proposed root `steamed`: []. Smooth egg custards cooked in bowls need a distinct form, shared by chawanmushi and steamed eggs. Avoid calling them soup.
- `breaded-cutlet`: [`fried-crisp`, `baked`]. Flattened breaded chicken, browned then baked under tomato and cheese; a flat cutlet rather than fried pieces or a generic casserole.
- `sweet-pudding`: [`sweets`, `steamed`]. Dense moulded steamed Christmas pudding, distinct from baked cake and custard.
- `yeast-bread`: [`bread-based`, `baked`]. Ciabatta is fermented bread, not quick bread.
- `caramelized-custard`: [`sweets`]. Set spoonable custard with brittle caramel top; not a tart without pastry. Specific heating variation is in steps/notes.
- `laminated-pastry`: [`bread-based`, `baked`]. Unfilled laminated croissant; `filled-pastry` would be false.
- `filled-pancake`: [`griddled`, `wrapped`, `sweets`]. Two griddled sponge pancakes sandwiching sweet bean paste, distinct from thin crepes or baked pastries.
- `confit-piece`: [`baked`]. Whole tender duck leg slowly cooked submerged in fat in the provided oven recipe, not slices, stew chunks or a crisp roast. The `confit` step preserves the defining process. Parent could choose a broader `fat-cooked` root instead; do not force this into roast-sliced.

Bases:

- `carrot`: [`vegetable`]. Main grated vegetable in cake/salad.
- `eggplant`: [`vegetable`]. Main vegetable alongside potato in Disanxian (200 g eggplant, 150 g potato; pepper is third component, omitted to honor one/two bases).

Steps:

- `proof`: yeast rise/rest that develops bread structure.
- `toast`: dry browning of sandwich bread.
- `clarify`: egg-white clarification and fine straining of consommé; not mere simmering.
- `laminate`: repeated enclosure/rolling/folding of butter into croissant dough.
- `cure`: overnight dry salt seasoning of duck before fat cooking; not a liquid marinade.
- `confit`: very gentle extended cooking immersed in fat, distinct from roast/braise.

Notes (all supported by ingredients/method; these have no graph parents): `beer`, `olive-oil`, `nut`, `carrot`, `vanilla`, `mushroom`, `spirits`, `yeast`, `mayonnaise`, `wine`, `caramel`, `honey`, `red-bean`.
