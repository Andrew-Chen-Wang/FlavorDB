# Manual vocabulary reconciliation

Read all10 complete notes files and all285 literal profiles across batches00–09, categories.ts, profiles.ts and graph.ts. Existing category ancestors are preserved; additions below are proposals for integration, not tracked edits. Flavor values remain editorial estimates. The notes-file coverage list records completed input reading.

## Same labels, same transformations

Baked-cake/small-cake/crumb-cake consolidate into cake: molded size and crumble topping remain in dish names/steps. Keep layer-cake for an assembled stack, baked-cheesecake for a set cheese custard, and baked-pudding for soaked soft bread. Cookies and biscuit use biscuit. Savory/small pancakes consolidate into pancake, without a sweets ancestor: sugar is a taste, and rosti and okonomiyaki must not inherit a sweet form. Filled-pancake remains a distinct assembled pocket/sandwich.

Rice rolls use one rice-roll form whether sushi or gimbap; vinegar seasoning belongs in flavor notes. Sweet-yeast-bread becomes yeast-bread, shared with enriched brioche and hot-cross buns already tagged that way. Proof and taste intensities carry the relevant distinctions. Griddled and baked flatbreads remain distinct. Folded-flatbread and layered-flatbread are synonymous for msemmen/scallion pancake; apply the separately flagged Laobing override for consistency. Wrapped burritos and open-folded tacos stay separate because a taco is not a closed parcel. Quesadilla stays filled-flatbread.

Pureed-dip/cold-dip/vegetable-spread merge into dip: blending, draining, roasting and simmering already explain their different processes. Dip has a sauce parent without a compulsory cold parent, because anchoïade is warmed. Generic sauce cannot be a root and a tagged form simultaneously. Establish root sauce, then pouring-sauce for chimichurri/dressing/marinara/bechamel/hollandaise/veloute; make-roux, emulsify and cooking steps distinguish construction. Meat ragout, raw relish and pouring custard retain real served-form differences. Roux is a process rather than an otherwise identical sauce shape.

Open-faced-toast and topped-toast become open-sandwich; toast remains a defining step when present. Plain unheated tomato bread must not inherit mandatory toast/bake. Garlic bread retains baked-bread because it is a reheated coated loaf rather than topped open slices. Small baked rolls remain baked-roll; yeast is not inferred for cassava-cheese starch rolls. Plain-flatbread is baked-flatbread for the assigned pita, and flatbread is griddled-flatbread for the assigned arepa; those are literal renames restricted to these reviewed proposals.

Chunky-soup and brothy-soup merge: the shared shape is solids in broth without claiming transparent consommé. Keep existing clear-soup unchanged, plus cold-creamy-soup and starch-thickened-soup: smooth cold emulsions, roux/purees and starch-thickened broths differ in how they eat. Pudding is renamed rice-pudding only because its sole reviewed use is rice pudding. Steamed Christmas pudding and figgy pudding share steamed-pudding; baked bread pudding stays distinct. Creme brulee and crema catalana share caramelized-custard; an inherent baked ancestor would wrongly prescribe one process to all caramel-topped custards.

## Avoid forced ancestry

Breaded chicken parmesan and tonkatsu share fried-cutlet under fried-crisp. Do not make baking an ancestor: parmesan baking remains explicit in its steps. Fried-filled-pastry is wrapped/fried-crisp without sweets, because cannoli/paczki and savory samosa all use it. Laminated-pastry likewise does not require sweets: butter lamination is the shape/process, and sweetness varies. Savory pies/tarts avoid the existing sweet pie/tart ancestors.

Use meat-dish for confit and whole roast as well as drained poached meat. Confit is not obligatorily baked, sliced or in sauce. Pot-au-feu can use poached-meat for its drained meat/vegetables while broth service is described in the review; no thick stew implied. Unfilled and liver dumplings use dough-dish rather than wrapped: neither encloses a filling. Liver dumplings also connect to soup for their served broth. Omelette and scrambled cod/potato use pan-cooked, not griddled or wok-fried by force. Root soft-dish permits mashed-dish without calling aligot soup/casserole. Whole seasoned/poached eggs use cooked-egg. Sashimi raw-slices maps to existing cold-sliced, which already covers fish via gravlax as well as meat.

## Bases and aromatics

All36 requested bases have direct culinary parent judgments. Nuts are their own culinary family; almond/hazelnut/peanut descend from it (peanut is botanically a legume, but this culinary family reflects nut-like taste and peanut-butter use). Chickpea/black-eyed-pea descend from beans. Mushroom is culinary vegetable; coconut/banana/palm fruit descend from fruit. Stock/oil/starch and distinct starchy roots remain independent families: do not pretend generic stock is a known meat species or yam/cassava is potato. No existing base ancestry changes are proposed.

Nut/nutty consolidate into nuts, and the single pastry aroma label becomes the existing bread note for generic baked-wheat flavor, without inferring butter. Floral is resolved to orange-blossom because the only assigned use explicitly names orange-blossom water. Specific almond/walnut, wine/sherry/rum, whole-wheat/buckwheat, black/white pepper and milk/cream remain distinct supported aromatics. Sugar-syrup adds no nonbasic flavor and is removed by a dish-specific exception instead of assigned a fictional synonym.

Puree and blend consolidate into blend. Keep grind/mince, whisk/whip and ferment/proof distinct: grain/poppy grinding differs from raw-meat fine chopping, gentle egg mixing differs from aeration, and souring/fermentation of kimchi/dosa differs from dough expansion. Correct meringue whisk to whip explicitly. Cooked-sugar set, rice crisping, tea-egg shell cracking and hot-oil finish are defining changes, not ordinary chopping/stirring added indiscriminately.

Sparse note sets are preferable to fabricated flavors: accept the plain steamed egg, gnocchi, hardtack, meringue, poached egg and sashimi exceptions, and rosewater-only lokum after removing the basic-sweetness note. Recipe/identity blockers already skipped by readers remain deferred.
