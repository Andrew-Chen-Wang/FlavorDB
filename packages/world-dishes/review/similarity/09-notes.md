- FORM `savory-pie`: [wrapped,baked]. Existing pie/tart leaves include sweets, wrong for spanakopita.
- FORM `layered-dessert`: [sweets,cold-dish]; custard/biscuit/fruit layers without pastry shell.
- FORM root `soft-dish`: []; leaf `mashed-dish`: [soft-dish]. Potato-cheese puree is neither soup nor casserole.

- FORM `rice-pudding`: [rice-dish,sweets]; distinguish sweet rice from savory porridge.

- FORM `cookies`: [baked,sweets]; `pancake`: [griddled]. Both distinguish shapes absent in existing leaves.
- BASE root `nuts`: []; `hazelnut`: [nuts]; `honey`: [sweets]; `black-eyed-peas`: [beans]; `spinach`: [vegetable]; root `yam`: []; `palm-fruit`: [fruit]; `veal`: [beef]; `semolina`: [wheat]; `buckwheat`: [grain]; `banana`: [fruit]. - NOTES `nuts`, `honey`, `palm-oil`, `wine`, `onion`, `olive-oil`, `coconut`, `vanilla`. These are supplied tastes beyond basic intensities; none is an unsupported heat inference.
